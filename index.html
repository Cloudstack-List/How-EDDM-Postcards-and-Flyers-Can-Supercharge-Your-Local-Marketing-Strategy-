What Is EDDM?

EDDM is a service offered by USPS that lets businesses send marketing materials to entire neighborhoods without needing names, email addresses, or complicated databases.

You choose the zip code or delivery routes, and the postal service delivers your flyer to every home or business on the list. For small businesses, this means you can:

  • Promote locally without expensive targeting tools
  • Send to real households—no spam folders involved
  • Keep costs down with lower postage rates
  • Stay top-of-mind in your own community

It’s smart, scalable, and surprisingly easy—especially if you team up with a local EDDM printing company in MI like In The Mix Print, which handles everything from design to delivery.

Why Print Still Matters in a Digital World?

We’ve all heard it—“print is dead.” But walk through your neighborhood and check the mail.

You’ll likely find pizza coupons, real estate flyers, salon openings, and maybe even a local hardware store promotion. Why? Because it works.

A well-designed postcard doesn’t just advertise—it introduces. It gives your brand a physical presence.

Unlike a digital ad that disappears in seconds, business flyers for mail campaigns often linger on kitchen counters, fridge doors, or work desks.

Postcards and flyers make your brand feel tangible and trusted. They promote events with clear calls to action, highlight offers without distraction, and drive real-world engagement—foot traffic, calls, and clicks. In a digital world, print feels personal again.

When to Use EDDM Postcards and Flyers?

Not every marketing effort needs to be nationwide. Some of the best campaigns are hyperlocal. Here are five perfect times to go with event postcards and flyers printing:

  • New Store Openings—Let locals know you’ve arrived with a strong headline, bold visuals, and an irresistible offer.
  • Seasonal Sales or Grand Re-openings—Holiday discounts, spring sales, or special hours? Send out reminders that convert.
  • Workshops, Classes, or Free Events—Promote community activities like fitness classes, open houses, or fundraisers with clear details.
  • Home-Based or Service Businesses—HVAC, landscaping, cleaning, tutoring—if you're targeting homes, EDDM ensures you reach the right ones.
  • Local Political or Nonprofit Campaigns— Share messages that matter most to voters or community stakeholders right where they live.

What Makes a Flyer or Postcard Actually Work?

Great design isn’t optional—it’s essential. Here's what your mailer needs to make an impact:

  • A headline that stops the eye—Make it bold, benefit-led, and clear.
  • Visuals that sell the story—Show your product, people, or service in action.
  • A compelling offer—Discounts, freebies, deadlines. Give people a reason to act now.
  • A simple, clear CTA—Call, visit, book, RSVP—whatever you want them to do.
  • Essential info—Phone, address, website, and social handles.

A quality printing partner can guide you through this. And yes—EDDM flyer printing requires exact specs, so it’s best to work with a pro.

Measuring Success (Without Clicks or Pixels)

Direct mail might not have dashboards like Google Ads, but there are still clever ways to measure performance:

  • Use unique promo codes for each campaign
  • Add a QR code leading to a campaign-specific landing page
  • Ask new customers how they heard about you at point-of-sale
  • Track foot traffic or calls after the mail drop

Over time, these insights help refine your message and sharpen your ROI.
